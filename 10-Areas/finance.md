## Mode

> Pick one — start light, upgrade only when the habit is automatic.
>
> **A. Monthly totals only** — once a month, write category totals. Minimum effort.
> **B. Manual transactions** — every purchase logged. Max control, hard to keep up.
> **C. Bank CSV import** — paste the export, agg by category. Compromise.

Current mode: **A**
Target mode: **C**

## Goals

- [ ] Safety cushion: <N> months of expenses on cash account
- [ ] Clear ceiling for "optional" spending categories (eating out, gifts, clothes)
- [ ] Monthly review with partner (15 min over coffee/tea)

## Expense categories

- `exp_housing` — rent / utilities / mortgage / repairs
- `exp_food` — groceries
- `exp_kid` — children: clothes, activities, school, toys
- `exp_health` — meds, doctors, pharmacy
- `exp_transport` — taxi, fuel, public
- `exp_subscriptions` — recurring digital subscriptions
- `exp_clothes` — adult clothing
- `exp_eating_out` — cafes / delivery / restaurants
- `exp_gifts` — birthdays, holidays
- `exp_other` — everything else
